## Caption (copy and paste)

NYC's subway is a giant space heater, and we're letting the heat float out of sidewalk grates 💨🔥

GrateHeat catches that warm tunnel air right at the grate, then a heat pump sends it to the public school next door. 179 NYC school buildings sit within 150 m of an underground line. We'd start with 19 that still burn heating oil.

Our model's middle case for one school with 4 modules:
🔥 ~580 MWh of heat a year
🏫 ~60% of the school's heating
🌍 ~123 t less CO₂ a year

Would you want this in your city? Tell us below 👇

## Posting tips

- **Cover:** choose the "GrateHeat" logo frame (around 0:21) or the "FREE HEAT" hook frame (0:01).
- **Trending audio (optional):** the reel comes with its own beat. To ride a trending sound too, open the Instagram editor, add the sound from the Reels audio browser, set it to about 15–25%, and keep the original audio at 100% so the sound effects still land.
- **Timing:** post when your audience is most active (Insights → Most active times). Reply to comments in the first hour.
- **Collab:** add your teammates (and your school or club account) as collaborators so the reel shows up on all your profiles.
- Turn on **auto-captions** for viewers watching muted.
