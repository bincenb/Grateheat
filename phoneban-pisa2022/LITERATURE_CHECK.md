## Where this study fits

- **Already known:** single-country studies of phone bans and well-being disagree (England null, South Australia small benefit). PISA 2022 analyses so far have looked at distraction, phone-separation anxiety (a phone-specific item), bedtime notifications and achievement.
- **Gap:** no cross-national test of school phone bans against a **validated, non-phone-specific health measure** (the HBSC Symptom Checklist), including **sleep-onset difficulty**, in nationally representative samples from several continents.
- **Why it matters:** many jurisdictions passed phone bans in 2023–2026 (e.g. the Netherlands, England guidance, several US states, Australia, Brazil 2025) and partly justified them on health grounds. Evidence on health outcomes is thin and conflicting.
