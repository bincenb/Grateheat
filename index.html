GrateHeat

Trains heat the air in NYC’s subway tunnels, and that heat escapes through sidewalk grates. GrateHeat catches it at the grate and sends it to a heat pump in the school next door.

What if the grates move more air, or less?

Grate air speed changes the answer more than anything else in our model. Move the sliders, or enter your own field measurements.

This is a single middle-value case of our model. The full version runs 20,000 combinations of the uncertain inputs (median 164 MWh per module). The fixed values are listed in calc.js. It assumes the school can use 130°F hot water, and older steam buildings can only use part of it.
